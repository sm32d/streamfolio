The app's one moment of showmanship. Appears over a blurred Settings screen; the DJ reads real headlines aloud with rotating intros ("Now spinning on 99.9 FM: …", "This just in: …").

```jsx
<RadioCard broadcast="Now spinning on 99.9 FM: Central banks hold rates steady. From Reuters." />
```

The five equalizer bars animate on independent linear tweens (350/280/420/310/390ms, reversing). This is the only component that uses ExtraBold weight and letter-spaced caps.
