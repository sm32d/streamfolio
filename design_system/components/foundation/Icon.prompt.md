One Material Symbols Rounded glyph — use it anywhere the Android app draws a Compose `Icon`.

```jsx
<Icon name="bookmark_add" size={18} fill={false} color="var(--sf-primary)" />
```

`fill={false}` is the `Icons.Outlined.*` equivalent; `fill` (default) is `Icons.Default.*`. Common glyphs in StreamFolio: home, search, bookmark, bookmark_add, settings, play_arrow, pause, skip_next, playlist_add, queue_music, auto_awesome, radio, rss_feed, wifi_off, library_books, gesture, hearing, folder, folder_special, add, close, done, delete, share, arrow_back, keyboard_arrow_right, keyboard_arrow_down, keyboard_arrow_up, tune, swipe, list, backup, email, trending_up, history, grid_view, view_list, info, bedtime.
